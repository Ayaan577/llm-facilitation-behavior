
DIRECTIVENESS ANNOTATION GUIDELINES
====================================

Task: For each facilitation move, assign a binary label:
  1 = DIRECTIVE    (the facilitator tells, instructs, or steers)
  0 = NON-DIRECTIVE (the facilitator asks, reflects, or holds space)

DIRECTIVE (label = 1):
  The facilitator explicitly directs the group's actions, assigns tasks,
  prescribes methods, or structures the process with commands/instructions.

  Linguistic markers:
  - Imperative forms:  "Write down...", "Sketch three concepts...",
                        "Start prototyping now."
  - Let's + action:    "Let's use affinity mapping.", "Let's move to..."
  - Requests:          "Can you each share one insight?",
                        "I want you to rank these ideas."
  - Method prescription: "Use the how-might-we template.",
                         "Apply the SCAMPER technique."
  - Time-boxing:       "We have 5 minutes for this.", "Let's time-box."
  - Role assignment:   "You be the note-taker.", "Each team picks a presenter."
  - Process steering:  "We need to move on.", "Now we do evaluation.",
                        "Let's converge on one idea."

NON-DIRECTIVE (label = 0):
  The facilitator uses open questions, reflections, paraphrasing,
  or exploratory prompts that leave the direction to the group.

  Linguistic markers:
  - Open questions:    "What feels most important to you?",
                        "How might we look at this differently?"
  - Reflections:       "It sounds like the team is leaning toward...",
                        "I'm hearing that cost is a concern."
  - Wondering:         "I wonder what assumptions we're making.",
                        "I'm curious what drew you to that direction."
  - Summaries:         "So far we've identified three pain points.",
                        "The key themes seem to be..."
  - Acknowledgment:    "That's an interesting perspective.",
                        "I appreciate you raising that."
  - Silence/space:     "Let's sit with that for a moment.",
                        "Take a minute to think before we continue."

EDGE CASES:
  - "Let's take a step back and think about..." → DIRECTIVE (steers process)
  - "What if we tried a different approach?"    → NON-DIRECTIVE (open question)
  - "Let's brainstorm" (no specific method)     → DIRECTIVE (process command)
  - "How might we..." (HMW question)            → NON-DIRECTIVE (open prompt)
  - "Can you elaborate on that?"                → NON-DIRECTIVE (follow-up)
  - "Can you write that on the whiteboard?"     → DIRECTIVE (specific action)

INSTRUCTIONS:
  1. Read each move in context-free isolation (ignore surrounding dialogue)
  2. Enter 1 or 0 in the 'human_label' column
  3. Use the 'notes' column for ambiguous cases
  4. Do NOT look at the directiveness_score or classifier_label columns
     while annotating — cover or hide those columns first
  5. Aim for ~15-20 minutes total (~10 seconds per move)
